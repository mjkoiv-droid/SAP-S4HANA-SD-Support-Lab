## 4. Global SAP SD Support Model

The proposed support model includes three support levels.

### Level 1 — Service Desk

- Receive and register incidents
- Collect relevant business information
- Perform initial troubleshooting
- Refer unresolved issues to functional support

### Level 2 — SAP SD Functional Support

- Investigate SAP SD process issues
- Analyze configuration and master data
- Review sales orders, deliveries, and billing
- Identify root causes
- Coordinate cross-module investigations
- Recommend resolutions
- Support testing and business communication

### Level 3 — Technical and Specialist Support

- Analyze technical defects
- Investigate custom developments
- Resolve complex integration problems
- Coordinate with SAP and external specialists
- Implement approved technical corrections

Actual support responsibilities depend on the customer organization and support agreement.

## 5. Global Regional Support

The operating model considers support across:

- APAC — Asia-Pacific
- EMEA — Europe, Middle East and Africa
- AMER — Americas

### Follow-the-Sun Support

A follow-the-sun operating model enables incidents to be handed over between regional teams.

Important requirements include:

1. Clear incident ownership
2. Documented investigation history
3. Business impact assessment
4. Consistent priority classification
5. Explicit ownership transfer
6. Agreed escalation procedures
7. SLA monitoring
8. Effective global communication

## 6. Practical Business Scenarios

### Scenario A — Global Incident Handover

A Japanese manufacturing operation experiences a billing problem.

The APAC SAP SD support team performs an initial investigation but cannot confirm the root cause before the regional support period ends.

The case is handed over to the EMEA team.

Key learning:

Structured handover documentation supports investigation continuity and reduces unnecessary repetition.

### Scenario B — End-User Training

A manufacturing organization introduces SAP S/4HANA Sales processes to regional users.

Training is prepared for:

- Sales representatives
- Sales support staff
- Shipping specialists
- Billing specialists
- Business key users

Key learning:

Effective training must explain business processes and provide practical system exercises.

### Scenario C — Knowledge Transfer

An SAP S/4HANA implementation project is preparing to transfer responsibility to a permanent support organization.

The receiving team must understand:

- Business processes
- Configuration decisions
- Master data dependencies
- Cross-module integration
- Known incidents and workarounds
- Support procedures
- Escalation arrangements

Key learning:

Knowledge transfer should be validated before operational responsibility is accepted.

### Scenario D — Continuous Improvement

SAP SD support reports show recurring pricing errors.

The support organization analyzes incident patterns and identifies possible improvements in pricing condition maintenance.

Key learning:

Support performance should be evaluated through both technical service metrics and business process outcomes.

## 9. Continuous Improvement Approach

The proposed improvement process follows these stages:

1. Identify recurring incidents or business process weaknesses.
2. Analyze available evidence.
3. Determine root causes.
4. Evaluate business impact.
5. Prepare improvement recommendations.
6. Obtain appropriate approvals.
7. Implement approved changes.
8. Validate the results.
9. Document lessons learned.
10. Monitor sustainability.

Improvements should be supported by reliable operational data.
